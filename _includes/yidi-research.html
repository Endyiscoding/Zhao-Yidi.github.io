Robust Inference under Local Distribution Shift in Wasserstein Distance

This paper studies the robustness of estimators to small departures of the true data-generating process from a reference model that defines the parameter of interest. We measure distributional departures using the quadratic Wasserstein distance, which captures movements of the observations in the sample space due to various factors like measurement errors. We consider local deviations within shrinking Wasserstein neighborhoods so that both sampling uncertainty and distributional bias matter asymptotically. Within this framework, we derive local asymptotic minimax bounds and construct estimators that attain the optimal trade-off between efficiency and robustness. Our results cover both regular parametric models and models defined by unconditional moment restrictions.
