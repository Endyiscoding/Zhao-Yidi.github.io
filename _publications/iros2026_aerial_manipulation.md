This work combines a demonstration-trained visuomotor Diffusion Policy with onboard LiDAR–inertial odometry and whole-body model predictive control for outdoor aerial manipulation. The policy proposes end-effector motion, while model predictive control coordinates the aerial base and arm to execute it. The reported peg-in-hole experiments include five successful indoor trials and three successful outdoor trials under mild wind and suitable lighting, without motion capture or outdoor retraining.
