AM-Bench is a modular simulation suite and benchmark for multirotor-based aerial manipulation policy learning. It includes underactuated, fully actuated, and overactuated embodiments; 12 tasks spanning contact, transport, and constrained interaction; configurable aerodynamic disturbances and actuator saturation; standard low-level controllers; and baseline policy-learning algorithms. The benchmark supports system-level evaluation of how embodiment, control, disturbances, and policy choices interact.
